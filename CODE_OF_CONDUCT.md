# Code of conduct

Zenith is a collection of public open source projects. This document sets the behavior expected of everyone who participates in them: contributors, maintainers, and anyone who reports issues, proposes changes, or takes part in discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Participate in a constructive, respectful way, assuming good faith and focusing on the technical topic at hand.
- Give and receive feedback on the work, not on the person. Critique ideas and code, and accept that others may weigh trade-offs differently.
- Accept decisions made by maintainers of the repository you are participating in, and use issues to discuss them when you disagree.
- Write issues, pull requests, and comments so that someone with no prior context can understand them.
- Respect other people's time: search before posting, keep threads on topic, and avoid unnecessary follow-ups.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable include:

- Insults, personal attacks, demeaning or dismissive language, and sustained hostility.
- Discriminatory language or behavior related to any personal characteristic.
- Sexualized language or attention, unwelcome advances, or any form of harassment.
- Publishing others' personal information, whether private or public, without their consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, including trolling, bad-faith arguments, and repeated off-topic posting.
- Reporting issues or submitting changes in bad faith, such as false vulnerability reports or intentionally harmful contributions.
- Insisting on public discussion of a security vulnerability or of a private conduct report.

## Scope

This code applies in all spaces managed by the Zenith projects, including:

- Issues, pull requests, discussions, and project boards of the repositories listed in the [product catalog](docs/projects.md).
- This repository's issues and pull requests.
- Any other space where someone is representing a Zenith project, such as an event or an external forum where they identify themselves as working on a Zenith project.

Behavior outside these spaces is not governed by this document, except when it affects the safety of participants or the integrity of a project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately. Do not open a public issue about it.

**Pending:** no dedicated contact address has been published for conduct reports. Until one is, reports can be sent through the same private channels listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md): GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository when enabled, or a plain issue requesting a private contact channel, without describing the incident publicly.

Reports are reviewed by the maintainers of the affected repository as capacity allows. Maintainers will:

- Acknowledge the report when communication is possible.
- Investigate the situation and decide on a response proportionate to the behavior.
- Communicate the outcome to the reporter, and to the person reported when appropriate.

Possible responses, depending on severity and repetition, include a private warning, removal of the offending content, temporary or permanent restriction on participation in the affected repository, or reporting the behavior to GitHub when it violates their terms.

Maintainers who witness or receive reports of unacceptable behavior are expected to act on them; where the report concerns a maintainer themselves, it should be handled by another maintainer of the same repository.

## Changes to this document

This document may be updated as the projects gain more maintainers or dedicated contacts. The version in this repository is the one that applies; significant changes should be proposed through a pull request to this repository, following [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
